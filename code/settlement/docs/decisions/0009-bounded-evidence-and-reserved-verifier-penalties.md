# ADR 0009: Evidence-aware appeal deadlines and reserved verifier penalties

Status: Accepted by the user on 2026-09-08; implementation and verification follow.

The user selected renewed appeal time after disclosure and conservative per-assignment penalty reservation. Initial evidence requests are limited to one per assignment. While requested evidence is pending, appeal, review selection and finalization are blocked. Valid disclosure starts a full new appeal period and resets the automatic candidate block to its end plus one. Failure voids the verdict, charges the late penalty and replaces that stage within its attempt limit. An assignment may use delivery recovery only once and only while its judgment is timely.

Each assignment reserves max(latePenalty, wrongPenalty) and retains purchaseValue + maxReviewCost liability until voiding or case finalization. Admission tests all liabilities against bond after every reserved penalty, using the most conservative cap and fraction encountered while that verifier has open liabilities. This conservative envelope resets only at zero open liability; it may remain stricter than the currently outstanding profiles. This bounds bookkeeping without weakening any outstanding promise. Exits require zero liability and zero penalty reserve. Every assignment in a case is unique and the history is bounded by three stages times maxAssignments.

Initial PASS/FAIL/INCONCLUSIVE all receive an evidence/appeal window; only buyer/PASS and seller/FAIL permit party appeal. Later deciding verdicts follow the existing exclusive PARTY/AUTO/TIEBREAK rules. Unavailable randomness or assignment exhaustion yields INCONCLUSIVE, never seller failure. Signed verdict encodings and cryptographic vectors are unchanged. A public result is not an off-chain predicate implementation.

The expanded conservative horizon includes two buyer recovery procedures (legacy buyer then quality conversion plus concurrent recovery), one serve cycle per assignment, renewed judgment after serve, initial evidence requests and renewed appeal windows, and two review seals with inclusive-block slack. Off-chain profile hashes and on-chain validation must agree. Two new immutable modules hold judgment/evidence and review logic; existing runtime selectors and storage prefix remain unchanged, while the deployment constructor takes eight module addresses. No upgrade path is added.

P1: purchase identity and atomic settlement unchanged. P2: concurrent recovery retains its own deposit and never releases the principal while open; terminal seller failure cancels all assignment liabilities/reserves and resolves an appeal deposit once. P3: full appeal opportunity follows disclosure; terminal INCONCLUSIVE is not FAIL. P4: bounded replacement and distinct participants preserve path assumptions, but reserved-capital exclusions can reduce effective detection and must be included in conditional probability bounds. No empirical detection lower bound is claimed.

A strike-limit removal excludes both ACTIVE and EXITING verifiers from future assignment. A REMOVED verifier may finalize exit directly only after its retention lock, zero liabilities and zero reservations; removal does not confiscate the remaining unslashed bond.
